VERA can't do.

Because VERA has already analyzed the deal, prepared your documents, and walked you through every step — the mortgage conversation takes 20 minutes, not 2 hours. You arrive knowing exactly what you're buying and why it works. I confirm the numbers and we move forward.

You are always free to use your own mortgage agent. VERA works with anyone.
