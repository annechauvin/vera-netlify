Before you connect
Create a folder in your Google Drive named exactly "VERA Financials", then create these subfolders inside it and upload the matching documents into each. VERA reads each step's documents from its own subfolder — nothing else in your Drive is visible to it.
Recommended folder structure
  • 2. 3 recent pay stubs
  • 6. Equifax or TransUnion credit report
  • 7. 90-day bank statement — full transaction detail, not just a balance (used for assets and debts)
  • 9. Assets (investment/RRSP statements)
  • 10. Loan and credit statements (for debts)
  • 11. Budget app category summary — export or screenshot from your budget app's expense breakdown
Not needed yet: ID, T4, T1 General, NOA, and employment letter — these are collected later, at pre-approval.
For later — pre-approval documents
  • 1. 2 pieces of ID
  • 3. 2-years T4 slips
  • 4. 2-years T1 General (if self-employed)
  • 5. 2-years NOA
  • 8. Employment letter
